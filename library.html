Library



“Knowledge is Power” powering this is a virtual encyclopedia collection forming the library. The library arms the students with all around knowledge with a collection of over 48000 volumes and 8300 titles with many national and E-journals Subscribed form time to time. A separate reference section is provided to facilitate the faculty and students to fulfill the requirements for project, seminar and research work. Automation of library and services to keep pace with ongoing technology changes the library record have been computerized for making easy access to the faculty and students and also save the time. A spacious reading hall is made available for students round the clock. In addition to Central Library a separate department Library helps for quick references to the respective needs.
