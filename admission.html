Eligibility

Candidate should have passed HSC (10+2) or its equivalent examination and the guidelines prescribed by Director of Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra. Diploma in Engineering with 45% marks are also eligible for FE admission. For further details please refer https://cetcell.mahacet.org/
