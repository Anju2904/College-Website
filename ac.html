ADMISSION NOTICE


Applications from the eligible and interested students for Provisional Registration against Institute level seats and probable vacancies arising out of Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) 2014-15 for First Year Engineering are invited.

Eligibility

Candidate should have passed HSC (10+2) or its equivalent examination and the guidelines prescribed by Director of Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra. Diploma in Engineering with 50% marks are also eligible for FE admission. For further details please refer www.dte.org.in
