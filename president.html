President and Secretary Message






We welcome you all warmly on behalf of Mahatma Basweshwar Education Society and M.S.Bidve Engineering College, Latur

Our mission is to achieve technical excellence with personality development so as to serve our society and the nation we work on the philosophy of Co-operation and honest involvement of the management and faculty to shape the future of the students.

With the experience of about five decades we have achieved excellence in technical and professional field, this institute has celebrated Silver Jubilee in the year 2008. We have been certified by ISO 9000 in the past & the institute was accredited by NBA in 2007

M.S.B.E.C. Latur is one of the best institute we have under our umbrella

We wish all the students a bright future.


Shri.S.M. Bidve
President
